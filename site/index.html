METHODOLOGY

What the system is allowed to conclude

Supported

Available evidence supports the material factual proposition using comparable definitions and dates.

Contradicted

Reliable evidence directly conflicts with the material factual proposition.

Missing context

A number or fact may be technically correct but omits information necessary to interpret it accurately.

Insufficient evidence

The available record is not strong enough for a defensible conclusion.

Not checkable

Opinion, prediction, rhetoric, value judgment, or a proposition without an objective verification test.

Pending

A checkable claim has been detected but the evidence packet has not been completed.

ClaimWatch evaluates the relationship between a public claim and available evidence. It does not infer a speaker's private intent from a contradiction.
